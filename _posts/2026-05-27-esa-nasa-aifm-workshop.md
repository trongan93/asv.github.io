## 🛰 2nd ESA-NASA Workshop on AI Foundation Models for Earth Observation

### 📢 Heading to Huntsville

I'm excited to attend the **2nd ESA-NASA Workshop on AI Foundation Models for Earth Observation**, hosted by **NASA Marshall Space Flight Center (MSFC)** in Huntsville, Alabama.

I'll be joining the EO and AI-FM community to learn, exchange ideas, and connect with researchers, engineers, and practitioners working on foundation models, onboard AI, data volume reduction, and operational Earth observation systems.

I'll also be presenting in the oral session:

> **Exploring embedding space for causality, science discovery, semantic data mining and data volume reduction**
> May 21 | 3:00–5:00 PM | Classroom A
>
> **Presentation:** Deployable Learned Compression for Satellite Imagery on MPSoC/FPGA Payloads Using Hardware-Aware Transforms and Bit-Exact Entropy Coding

### 🎤 Recap: A Great Week in Huntsville

While working on the **Lilium project**, a CubeSat platform, one of the key questions we explored was how the global space community and international research teams were advancing onboard AI for foundation models in space applications.

During that time, I came across the **European Space Agency (ESA) Φ-lab** onboard AI initiatives, which became an important reference for our work. Since then, I've continued following the Φ-lab team's publications and activities through LinkedIn.

Last week, I had the opportunity to attend the workshop in person. It was an exciting and inspiring experience to gain deeper insight into the innovative work being carried out by the ESA Φ-lab team, as well as to exchange ideas with experts and researchers in this rapidly evolving field.
