## 以人工智慧驅動之酬載提升衛星數據壓縮與遙感技術

On **February 24, 2025**, Dr. **Andrew (Trong-An) Bui**, Assistant Professor at the Institute of Aerospace and System Engineering, NTUT, gave an invited talk to the **Department of Electrical Engineering, National Taipei University of Technology (NTUT)**, hosted by **Lab 414 — the High Performance Computing and Deep Learning Lab / NVIDIA-NTUT Embedded GPU Joint Laboratory**.

The talk, **"Enhancing Satellite Data Compression and Remote Sensing with AI-Driven Payloads"** (以人工智慧驅動之酬載提升衛星數據壓縮與遙感技術), covered how deep learning-based payload processing can enhance onboard satellite data compression and downstream remote sensing performance — from efficient deep learning models and satellite-friendly motion estimation to CubeSat, UAV, and SWIR edge-device research projects bridging embedded AI acceleration with practical Earth observation applications.
