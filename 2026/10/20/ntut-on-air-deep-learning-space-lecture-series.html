🎬 課程錄影公開上線

由裴重恩老師主講的 《深度學習與太空科技應用》（Deep Learning in Space Technology Applications） 全系列課程錄影，已由 國立臺北科技大學 NTUT ON AIR 製作完成並公開於 YouTube，共 6 講，每講約 50 至 70 分鐘。

課程內容涵蓋神經網路基礎、進階深度學習架構，以及在太空資料分析與自主系統上的實際應用。無論是本校同學、其他學校的學生，或是對星載 AI 與遙測影像處理有興趣的讀者，都可以免費線上觀看。

🎬 Full Lecture Series Now Online

The complete lecture series for Deep Learning in Space Technology Applications, taught by Dr. Trong-An Bui, has been produced by NTUT ON AIR at National Taipei University of Technology and is now publicly available on YouTube — six lectures of roughly 50 to 70 minutes each.

The course covers the fundamentals of neural networks, advanced deep learning architectures, and their practical application to space data analysis and autonomous systems. Whether you are a Taipei Tech student, a student elsewhere, or simply interested in onboard AI and remote-sensing image processing, the whole series is free to watch.
