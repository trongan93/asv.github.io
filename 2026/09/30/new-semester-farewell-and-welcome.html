🎓 新學期，新成員

新學期正式開始。對實驗室來說，這也是一個交接的時刻：我們向今年畢業的同學道別，同時歡迎新加入的夥伴。

感謝畢業的同學

謝謝 張銘介、陳韋銘、黃耘宣 三位今年自北科大電子所畢業的同學。從影像壓縮、超解析到 FPGA 上的模型實現，你們在實驗室的每一段研究，都成為後續工作的基礎。感謝你們曾經是實驗室的一份子，祝福大家在下一個階段一切順利。

歡迎新成員

歡迎 張天佑、呂聖韡、許詠駿、陳樂恩、王奕程 五位加入北科大太空系統工程研究所的新同學。期待與你們一起，在星載 AI、多感測器融合與衛星通訊等方向繼續往前走。

🎓 A New Semester, New Faces

The new semester is underway, and for the lab that means a handover: we say goodbye to this year’s graduates and welcome the members who are just joining.

Thank you to our graduates

Our thanks to Ming-Chieh Chang, Wei-Ming Chen and Yun-Hsuan Huang, who completed their M.S. in Electronic Engineering at Taipei Tech this year. From image compression and super-resolution to deploying those models on FPGA, the work you did here is what the next projects are built on. Thank you for being part of the lab, and all the best for what comes next.

Welcome to our new members

A warm welcome to Tien-Yu Chang, Sheng-Wei Lu, Yung-Chun Hsu, Le-En Chen and Yi-Cheng Wang, who join us from the Graduate Institute of Space Systems Engineering. We look forward to pushing ahead together on onboard AI, multi-sensor fusion and satellite communications.
