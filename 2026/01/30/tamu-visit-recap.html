🇹🇼🤝🇺🇸🚀 Looking Back at Our Visit to Texas A&M University

Looking back at our visit last November to Texas A&M University with the team from National Taipei University of Technology (NTUT)—and excited about how that visit has already turned into real progress.

We were warmly hosted by Dr. Daniel Selva at the Department of Aerospace Engineering, along with the Department Head and many wonderful faculty members. The open discussions, lab tours, and exchange of ideas laid a strong foundation for collaboration.

Since then, we’ve been actively co-working to deepen and strengthen the collaboration between National Taipei University of Technology and Texas A&M—turning conversations into concrete plans and meaningful partnerships in aerospace, space systems, and engineering education.

Grateful for the trust, teamwork, and shared vision. Looking forward to building even more together in the next phase of this collaboration!

A special thank you to the Texas A&M Aerospace Engineering research assistants and students who guided us through so many lab visits and shared their work with us—your enthusiasm and hospitality truly made the visit special. Thanks, Joshua Elston, David Fornos Fernandez, Priyank Dubey, Kazuki Toma, Raul Santos, and Alexander Crum.
