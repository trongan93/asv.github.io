{%- comment -%} Formats an integer with thousands separators, e.g. 13600000 -> 13,600,000. Liquid has no such filter, and the alternative was storing every grant amount twice (once summable, once pre-formatted), which drifts. Usage: {% include number.html value=grant.amount %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign digits = include.value | round | append: "" -%} {%- assign len = digits.size -%} {%- assign head = len | modulo: 3 -%} {%- if head == 0 -%}{%- assign head = 3 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign out = digits | slice: 0, head -%} {%- assign groups = len | minus: head | divided_by: 3 -%} {%- for i in (1..groups) -%} {%- if groups < 1 -%}{%- break -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign start = i | minus: 1 | times: 3 | plus: head -%} {%- assign chunk = digits | slice: start, 3 -%} {%- assign out = out | append: "," | append: chunk -%} {%- endfor -%} {{- out -}}
