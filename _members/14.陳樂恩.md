北科大太空所碩士生，指導老師 裴重恩 教授

**陳樂恩**目前就讀於國立臺北科技大學太空系統工程研究所，大學畢業於電子工程學系，具備嵌入式系統開發經驗。目前研究方向為人工智慧於衛星影像處理之應用，主要關注深度學習演算法在遙測影像超解析上的發展，以及其於後續影像判釋任務的應用潛力。

**Le-En Chen** is a *Master's student in the Graduate Institute of Space Systems Engineering at National Taipei University of Technology*. He holds a bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering and has a background in embedded systems development. His research applies deep learning to satellite imagery, with a focus on super-resolution for remote sensing data and how it can support downstream image interpretation.
