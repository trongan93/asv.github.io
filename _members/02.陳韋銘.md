北科大電子所碩士，2026 年畢業，共同指導老師 林信標 教授

**陳韋銘**於 2026 年畢業於國立臺北科技大學電子工程系研究所，碩士期間在林信標教授與裴重恩助理教授的聯合指導下，進行基於深度學習技術的FPGA(現場可程式化邏輯閘陣列)影像壓縮設計。

他的研究領域為數位影像處理與FPGA開發，具備使用C語言與Verilog HDL進行SoC（單晶片系統）設計的實作經驗。其研究計畫著重於結合深度學習演算法與嵌入式系統，並應用於低軌衛星的影像壓縮技術。

**Wei-Ming Chen** earned his *M.S. in Electronic Engineering from National Taipei University of Technology, Taiwan, in 2026*. His thesis research focused on FPGA-Based Image Compression Design using Deep Learning Techniques, under the supervision of **Professor Hsin-Piao Lin** and **Assistant Professor Chung-En Pei** (co-advisor).

In parallel with his academic pursuits, Wei-Ming Chen gained practical experience in System-on-Chip (SoC) design using both C programming and Verilog HDL. His research project focused on integrating deep learning algorithms into embedded systems, particularly for image compression applications in low-earth-orbit (LEO) satellite imagery.
