北科大太空所碩士生，指導老師 裴重恩 教授

**張天佑**，國立臺北科技大學太空系統工程研究所碩士生，指導教授為裴重恩（Trong-An Bui）教授。大學畢業於資訊工程學系，具備程式設計與軟硬體整合能力。研究方向聚焦於 AI Agent 與基於深度學習的影像壓縮，並致力於將模型部署於 FPGA 嵌入式平台。目前參與國科會立方衛星計畫，負責衛星影像的在軌邊緣運算。

**Tien-Yu (Solomon) Chang** is a *master's student at the Graduate Institute of Space Systems Engineering, National Taipei University of Technology (Taipei Tech)*, under the supervision of Dr. Trong-An Bui. He received his bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Information Engineering and has solid skills in programming and hardware–software integration. His research focuses on AI agents and deep learning–based image compression, with an emphasis on deploying models on FPGA-based embedded platforms. He is currently part of a CubeSat project funded by the National Science and Technology Council (NSTC), where he is responsible for in-orbit edge computing of satellite imagery.
