北科大太空所碩士生，指導老師 裴重恩 教授

**王奕程**目前就讀於國立臺北科技大學太空系統工程研究所碩士班，大學畢業於通訊工程學系。他熟悉天線設計、HFSS 與實作量測，且具備 MATLAB／Simulink 的訊號處理與系統模擬經驗。

**Yi-Cheng Wang** is a *Master's student in the Graduate Institute of Space Systems Engineering at National Taipei University of Technology*. He received his bachelor's degree in Communication Engineering. He is experienced in antenna design, HFSS simulation, and hands-on measurement, and has experience in signal processing and system simulation using MATLAB/Simulink.
