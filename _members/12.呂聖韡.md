北科大太空所碩士生，指導老師 裴重恩 教授

**呂聖韡**目前就讀於國立臺北科技大學太空系統工程研究所碩士班，大學畢業於電機工程學系通訊組。在裴重恩教授的指導下，他的研究聚焦於基於深度學習的影像辨識技術，涵蓋物件偵測、分類與追蹤，並以無人機（UAV）與衛星影像為主要應用場域。

他熟悉 Python 與 PyTorch、TensorFlow 等深度學習框架，亦具備 MATLAB／Simulink 的訊號處理與系統模擬經驗。他對於提升模型在複雜環境下的辨識準確度與穩健性抱有濃厚興趣，並期望將所開發的演算法延伸至航太領域的實際應用。

**Sheng-Wei Lu** is a *Master's student in the Graduate Institute of Space Systems Engineering at National Taipei University of Technology*. He received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electrical Engineering, specializing in communications. Under the supervision of Dr. Trong-An Bui, his research focuses on deep learning-based image recognition, covering object detection, classification, and tracking, with UAV and satellite imagery as the primary application domains.

He is proficient in Python and deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch and TensorFlow, and has experience in signal processing and system simulation using MATLAB/Simulink. He is particularly interested in improving the accuracy and robustness of recognition models in complex environments, and aims to extend the algorithms he develops to real-world aerospace applications.
