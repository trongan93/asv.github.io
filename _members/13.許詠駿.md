北科大太空所碩士生，指導老師 裴重恩 教授

**許詠駿**目前就讀於國立臺北科技大學太空系統工程研究所碩士班，研究興趣主要聚焦於雷達、影像感測以及多感測器融合技術。他大學畢業於電機工程學系通訊組，具備無線通訊與天線設計相關背景，曾參與無人機地空通訊系統研究，進行 LoRa 通訊、天線設計、電磁模擬與實作量測，並累積射頻與無線系統相關經驗。

目前他進一步投入雷達與相機感測融合相關研究，提升目標偵測與環境感知能力。其研究興趣亦涵蓋無人機與太空系統中的感測應用、雷達感知、影像辨識，以及人工智慧於多模態感測資料整合上的應用，希望結合過去的通訊與天線背景，進一步發展智慧感測與太空系統相關技術。

**Yung-Chun Hsu** is a *Master's student in the Graduate Institute of Space Systems Engineering at National Taipei University of Technology*, with research interests in radar, image sensing, and multi-sensor fusion. He received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electrical Engineering, specializing in communications, and has a background in wireless communication and antenna design. He previously took part in research on UAV air-to-ground communication systems, working on LoRa communication, antenna design, electromagnetic simulation, and hands-on measurement, and gained experience in RF and wireless systems.

He is now working on radar–camera sensor fusion to improve target detection and environmental perception. His research interests also include sensing applications in UAVs and space systems, radar perception, image recognition, and the use of artificial intelligence for multimodal sensor data integration. Building on his background in communications and antennas, he aims to further develop intelligent sensing and space system technologies.
