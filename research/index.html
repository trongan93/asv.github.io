Research

AeroSat Vision Lab 致力於神經網路、電腦視覺與遙測技術在航太與衛星應用領域的前瞻研究。我們專注於太空載具之人工智慧自主系統開發，特別是以 OrbitAI 為核心的嵌入式 AI 平台。

AeroSat Vision Lab advances neural networks, computer vision, and remote sensing for aerospace and satellite systems. Our work centres on AI-driven autonomy for spacecraft — combining deep learning, embedded computing, and space-grade sensor integration to change how intelligent systems operate in orbit.

研究方向 Research Areas

Onboard & Embedded AI星載人工智慧

Real-time inference within the power, memory, and radiation budgets of a satellite payload — quantised models deployed to FPGA, MCU, and DPU targets. This is the core of the OrbitAI platform.

2 funded programs

SAR & Optical FusionSAR 與光學影像融合

Using all-weather synthetic aperture radar to restore optical imagery obscured by cloud, with cross-modality co-registration and attention-guided reconstruction — applied to disaster response and carbon stock verification.

2 funded programs

Detection & Tracking目標偵測與追蹤

Maritime and ground target surveillance from orbit, including deep reinforcement learning for UAV trajectory design across integrated space–air–sea networks.

2 funded programs

LEO Communications & Security低軌通訊與資安

Compression and downlink efficiency for constrained links, alongside anomaly detection, disinformation detection, and resilience for low-Earth-orbit satellite communication systems.
